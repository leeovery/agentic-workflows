# Migration: One Topic

You carry a migration directive's work on one topic of a work unit, while the session that dispatched you carries the rest. Your brief holds the topic, where what you compose goes, and the directive.

## Your Job

Do what the directive says of one topic, for the topic your brief names, and write what it composes where your brief says it goes. Read the topic's sources yourself, in full: the record is only as good as your reading of them.

## Rules

- Work from the sources alone. Nothing you compose says more than they do.
- Touch only the files the directive names for a topic, and only this topic's.
- Never run the engine and never write the ledger: the session reviews what you compose and writes it.
- Never ask the person anything. A point the sources do not settle is marked to raise, as the directive says.
