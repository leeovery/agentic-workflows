# An epic and its topics

An epic is what a piece of work becomes when it turns out to be several things wearing one name. Confirming one doesn't end the conversation; it opens it up into a proper design session, with the system playing the part of a second senior engineer who is willing to disagree, and who writes down not only what was decided but what was considered and dropped. Only when you say the ground is covered does it draw out the topics, and each topic leaves with a brief carrying the decisions, the rejected paths and the open questions that concern it.

From there you live with the epic through its dashboard, which is arranged in three bands. Discovery is where you explore and decide, Definition is where those decisions become a specification and a plan, and Delivery is where the work is built and checked. Each topic makes its own way down through the bands at its own pace, and the menu beneath the dashboard always points at the most sensible next move.

```
   ── DISCOVERY ─────────────────────────────────────────────
      ├─ ✓ sign-in             decided
      ├─ ◐ billing             discussing
      └─ ○ the basic reports   fresh · routed to research
   ── DEFINITION ────────────────────────────────────────────
      └─ sign-in               specification, then planning
   ── DELIVERY ──────────────────────────────────────────────
      └─ sign-in               implementation · phase 2 of 3
```
