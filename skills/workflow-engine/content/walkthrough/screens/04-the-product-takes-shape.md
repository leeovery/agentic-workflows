# The product takes shape

Quite often that first conversation turns out not to be about a piece of work at all. It's about the product: what it is, what has to exist before launch, what can wait until there's revenue. Rather than forcing a project into existence too early, the system stays at that altitude with you. You talk, it listens for the way you stage things, and when you ask it to lay it all out it proposes a roadmap: the capabilities you described as items, grouped into horizons you name yourself, in the order you mean them. From then on, whenever an idea surfaces mid-conversation that belongs to a later release, one line puts it on the roadmap and you carry on without losing your thread.

Work begins the moment you pick items to start. Several items usually become an epic, a single one becomes a feature, and the new work is fenced to just those items while everything else stays visibly waiting its turn.

```
   ── launch ────────────────────────────────────────────────
        sign-in          billing          the basic reports
   ── v1 ────────────────────────────────────────────────────
        exports          team accounts
   ── someday ───────────────────────────────────────────────
        marketplace

        pick items to start ──→ an epic, fenced to just those
```
