# The idea

*About five minutes, and you can leave whenever you like.*

Claude Code is a genuinely capable engineer with two gaps: it forgets everything between sessions, and it has no particular process for how a piece of work should unfold. These workflows fill both. The habit they bring is that thinking gets written down before anything gets built. What the work actually is, what was decided and why, exactly what will be made, and in what order all become documents in your repository, and the code is built from those documents rather than from an improvised conversation. Alongside that sits a memory, so that once a piece of thinking is finished it stays available to every piece of work that comes after it.

```
      you and Claude talk it through, and the thinking
      is written down before anything is built

        what the work is · what was decided, and why
        exactly what will be made · in what order
                            ↓
          agents build it from those documents
                            ↓
      the memory keeps what was learned, so the next
      piece of work starts warmer than the last
```

Throughout, the division of labour stays the same: the decisions are yours, and keeping the record straight and doing the work is the system's. Everything you're about to see follows from that.
