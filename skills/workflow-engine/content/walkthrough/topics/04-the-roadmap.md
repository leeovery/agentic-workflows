# The roadmap

Some conversations are about the product rather than a piece of work: a new product being conceived, what the next few months should hold, a v2 taking shape. The roadmap is where those live. It sits above the pieces of work and holds everything you have decided on but not yet started: ideas, bugs and quick-fixes.

It has two parts. Items are what it holds, each marked as an idea, a bug or a quick-fix: an idea is a capability at the grain you would move around a real roadmap, a bug or a quick-fix is one fix, and each has a one-line summary and a pointer back to the conversation or inbox note it came from. Horizons are the buckets they sit in, named in your own staging words, and their order is the meaning: the first horizon is what comes next. A horizon you call Backlog or Later is your backlog; the roadmap has no other.

```
   ── launch ───────────────────────────────────────────────
        items you need before anything ships
   ── v1 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────
        items for the release after that
   ── someday ──────────────────────────────────────────────
        items you want on record but not yet
```

The roadmap comes into being the first time something lands on it, in any of four ways. The deliberate way is a conversation from the start menu that turns out to be about the product as a whole; the system recognises that, confirms it, and carries on at that level, and when you ask it to lay it out it proposes the items and horizons it heard. The incidental way is a park: mid-way through any other conversation you place something yourself ("that's a v2 thing", "fix that before launch") and it goes straight onto the roadmap in the horizon you named, as an idea, a bug or a quick-fix, while the conversation continues. The third way is a topic leaving: one inside an epic that turns out to belong to a later release is postponed onto the roadmap rather than cancelled, and where there is no roadmap yet it is created with it. The fourth is the inbox: notes you decide to do, but not now, go onto the roadmap under a horizon you name, each note travelling with its item.

Work begins when you start work on items. Pick one, several or a whole horizon from the roadmap's menu, and a piece of work is born fenced to exactly those items, taking the largest shape among them. Ideas usually become an epic and the conversation flows straight into shaping it, or a feature when they read as one coherent build; bugs with no idea among them become a bugfix, and quick-fixes alone a quick-fix. Bugs and quick-fixes riding along with ideas never add size, and a bug that rides along skips the investigation it would get on its own. Whatever you did not pick stays visibly waiting. Once an item is being built you can watch it from the roadmap but not move or remove it, because redirecting work in flight is that work's decision; if the work is cancelled, the item returns to waiting with its history intact.

Once a roadmap exists the start menu shows it, and the `r/roadmap` row takes you in to talk about the product, add or re-sort items, or start work on the next slice.
