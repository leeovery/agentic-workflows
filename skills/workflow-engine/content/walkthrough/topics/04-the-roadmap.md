# The roadmap

Some conversations are about the product rather than a piece of work: a new product being conceived, what the next few months should hold, a v2 taking shape. The roadmap is where those live. It sits above the pieces of work and holds everything you have shaped but not yet committed to build.

It has two parts. Items are capabilities at the grain you would move around a real roadmap, each with a one-line summary and a pointer back to the conversation that produced it. Horizons are the buckets they sit in, named in your own staging words, and their order is the meaning: the first horizon is what comes next.

```
   ── launch ───────────────────────────────────────────────
        items you need before anything ships
   ── v1 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────
        items for the release after that
   ── someday ──────────────────────────────────────────────
        items you want on record but not yet
```

The roadmap comes into being the first time something lands on it, in either of two ways. The deliberate way is a conversation from the start menu that turns out to be about the product as a whole; the system recognises that, confirms it, and carries on at that level, and when you ask it to lay it out it proposes the items and horizons it heard. The incidental way is a park: mid-way through any other conversation you place a capability yourself ("that's a v2 thing") and it goes straight onto the roadmap in the horizon you named, while the conversation continues.

Work begins when you start work on items. Pick one, several or a whole horizon from the roadmap's menu, and a piece of work is born fenced to exactly those items: several usually become an epic and the conversation flows straight into shaping it, a single coherent item becomes a feature. Whatever you did not pick stays visibly waiting. Once an item is being built you can watch it from the roadmap but not move or remove it, because redirecting work in flight is that work's decision; if the work is cancelled, the item returns to waiting with its history intact.

Once a roadmap exists the start menu shows it, and the `r/roadmap` row takes you in to talk about the product, add or re-sort items, or start work on the next slice.
