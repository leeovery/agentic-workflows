# Your task

Verify one record, a topic's or a unit's own, composed for the migration's records step, against its sources, before it is carried into the ledger. Its sources are those the brief below lists, and no other: never another topic's log, brief or record, nor another unit's, nor the project's code. The other topics the brief below lists, with what each is about, are for judging whose a point is, never sources. Hunt the record for detail it dropped, and for an entry its source does not bear out.

Walk the sheet below section by section: it lays the record against its sources, each section of each file with its lines and every entry citing it. Read each section in its source, then check that what each entry listed beneath it takes from the section is what the section says, and that each current decision, finding and open question the section holds is carried, by an entry listed there or elsewhere, or is not this record's; of the discovery sessions and the unit's assets, each point bearing on its subject, or for a unit's own record on no topic's subject. That a cited heading exists is no check. Check that each point a section leaves unmeasured, untested, unverified or to check is carried, a ruling setting it aside only where a later section measured it or made it moot, or a scope note, wherever it stands, put it out of scope; and that where a section contradicts another on a point no later one settles, the record names the contradiction. Walk every open section of the sheet, every section in a first round: a closed one was walked in an earlier round, every entry citing it passed unchanged, and none that cited it then gone from it, and is no finding; a section an entry stopped citing is open again, and your findings are every gap the walk turns up, never a sample. Check each decision, finding and open question against its later answers, as the directive's **Current answers only** below says: one carried as the earlier word has it, in whole or in part, is a finding.

The record passes when it holds each current decision, finding and open question its sources hold, as the directive says of it, and nothing material is missing or misstated:

- each entry is true to the section it cites;
- each section it cites of a spec drawing on it is carried whole: each current decision, finding and open question of it that concerns this record is carried; one it cites of a spec it does not draw on, the later answer, gives that answer alone;
- an entry crediting the person holds a line of its source where they decided, in those words where they handed the call over;
- no decision carries a side of a contradiction the record names, whatever question it answers: a point at odds is settled on neither side;
- nothing is carried twice — in a later round, judged on what changed since alone;
- nothing is carried that you rule is not this record's: such an entry is a finding, to remove, with the ruling beside it.

In a later round, check each finding answered, each entry or line changed since true to its source, and that no entry the sheet lists as removed since the last round took a point still to carry; raise nothing on what an earlier round passed unchanged. Where earlier rounds' findings and rulings below name anything, check that each finding is answered and each ruling kept. A point an earlier round's rulings set aside, or the session settled, is closed: it is raised again only where a line of its source that ruling or settlement missed says otherwise, and that finding is a reversal, opening `Reverses round {R}:`, R the round that ruled, or `Reverses the session:`, naming that line. A new point on what an earlier round passed, or on what its findings changed, is a finding as any other, never a reversal.

Wording, order, and how a section's points are shared out among entries are the composer's.

Write the findings file the brief names afresh: each finding one list item, the section of the source and what the record misses or misstates, or the file empty where the record passes — an empty file is the pass. Where you ruled that a point of the sources is not this record's, or is replaced by a later answer, in whole or in part, naming what still stands, write each such ruling into the rulings file the brief names, one line each, naming the point and its source's section. Write the checked file the brief names afresh, a line for each entry of the record the sheet does not mark passed unchanged, by its ref: `{ref}: ok`, or `{ref}: see finding` where a finding names it. Hand back whether it passes, and how many findings you wrote.
