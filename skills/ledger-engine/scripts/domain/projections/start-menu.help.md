The start menu is where every piece of work begins and resumes.

# → A number continues that piece of work: a feature, bugfix or quick-fix goes straight to where it stands, an epic opens its menu; the ones with something waiting on you come first. The last, while any is left, sorts the files the migration found no place for in Ledger: what holds knowledge never brought in joins the record, and the rest is kept as it is or discarded, as you choose.
p → Product carries on the conversation about the product as a whole: what it is, who it serves and where it is going.
s → Start begins something new: describe it, and the kind of work is settled as we talk.
t → Triage works through the inbox, the things you noted down to come back to: work on them now, put them on the roadmap, or archive them.
r → The roadmap holds what you've shaped but not yet committed to build, by horizon.
e → Verdicts appears while a call you changed waits for your word on what Claude's answer would have cost.
v → View lists completed and cancelled work, each opening in manage.
m → Manage pivots, absorbs, cancels or brings back a piece of work, and changes the project's setup, its drivers and the matrix.
