# Putting a Point

*Shared reference. Loaded by `ledger-discussion-process`, at its `## Instructions` section: how a point is put to the person in the conversation — a call of your own that the ask rule sends to them, a background review's finding, a synthesis's tension, or a message sent from another topic.*

---

A point put to the person is an opener, never a case: its whole job is to put the person in front of the problem and say where you stand. Everything else — the record's full case, your own supporting analysis, the costs, secondary consequences — stays back and enters the conversation as responses, when the person's reply calls for it. The source — the call you raised, the finding's report section, the message — is never the script: digest it, and digest it upward — a problem written in code is retold as what the product does, at the level [altitude.md](altitude.md) sets, whatever level the source chose. A mechanism is retold as the behaviour it produces, wherever in the point it appears; its tuning — a weight, a threshold, a window — stays in the record.

A call the ask rule sends to the person is put this way too: the raise's rows are its sides, and your lean its position — held back whole on a `blind`.

Three beats, composed in order.

## The Problem

Made immediately graspable, from zero. The product's situation opens — what its user meets and what happens to them — before anything else: the first sentence names the product's user and their moment, and names no report, agent, finding, mailbox, or topic — this topic's own name included: the product and its user are the point's subjects, and a work unit's or topic's slug never stands in for either. Where the problem came from rides in a clause once the situation is on the page — the background review and what it observed; the synthesis's two positions in tension; for a message, the topic it came from and what it asks of this topic — another topic's settled ground, or a decision it could not make without this. A point that opens on the report being back, on the mailbox, or on what another topic looked at, has opened on the wrong thing. Make the problem land before your position arrives: one to three devices, technical depth only as deep as seeing the problem needs — the devices and the test are [making-it-land.md](making-it-land.md)'s, in context.

Every point is a cold start — it lands in a corner of the record the person last held fully hours or days ago, or on ground another topic's session worked while this topic sat — so rebuild what seeing the problem requires, and nothing more. Restate any term borrowed from another point, another thread, or an earlier decision; never reference it bare. Never use a bare id (`F5`, `T2`, `003`) as a label in conversational prose — name a finding by its report title and a message by its title on first mention, or describe it by what it is; ids belong in the log and the entries, not in the conversation. When an earlier point from this set has gone before — the report's findings, the mailbox's messages — a one-line bridge sits above the opener: what the previous one settled — or simply that it was put, when that engagement predates this session — and how many follow this one (the review's: how many of its findings still need the person; the mailbox: what is still waiting behind this one — the asks left in a message, then the messages after it). The bridge, and a one-line map of a message's several asks, are the only lines that precede the problem; its first sentence follows them.

## The Position

Always, but on a `blind`. Where you stand and the one load-bearing reason that carries it — a clause, not the derivation, saying what the lean buys the product's user; the knobs the record tunes it by (a weight, a window, a threshold) stay back, named or numbered — at the firmness the answer has actually earned. Where the position leans against an alternative, that alternative gets **at most one clause naming the kind of cost it carries** — never two costs, never a cost with its consequence spelled out, never its size or its knobs (the window, weight, or threshold stays in the record, named or numbered), whether the cost comes from the record or from your own reading. A clause that enumerates or explains has become the case, and the case stays back. An option the record already rejected is not an alternative the position leans against: it stays back whole — unnamed, uncounted — with the rest of the case, and enters only as a response when the person reaches toward it.

On a `blind`, the position is held back whole: say nothing of your lean until they have answered, and the point closes on the question.

**Firmness** — judged live against the session, never read off the record. Where re-derivation moves the problem itself — it holds, it's narrower than framed, a decision made since the record was written already covers it — that is part of the position; say it. Then match the register to how determined the answer is:

- **One defensible shape** — a decision is owed and re-derivation can't find one to make: say so openly, propose it plainly — a cost worth knowing rides in a clause — and offer to lock it in unless the person has something to add. Still a presentation, never a demotion: the person sees it whole and can push back before anything lands.
- **A preferred path among real options** — name your pick and its reason; the alternatives get a clause each naming the kind of cost they carry, and nothing after it — never an option survey. The close points back at the pick's stated reason as what a push-back would have to move — it introduces no new material.
- **Genuinely open** — the person holds what the record lacks (a point that rests on the person's own knowledge is this by construction): say what you'd need to know and ask for it — the point's one genuine question lives here.
- **Needs investigation** — an experiment, a deep dive or a spike beats either of you guessing; suggesting it is the position.
- **No lean at all** — rare and honest: say so flat and ask for the person's read. Never manufactured; reach it only when re-derivation truly leaves nothing.

The position answers the problem's own question, never its bookkeeping: proposing to park, defer, forward, or record it as open is not a position — deferral is an outcome the person may choose, never one the point proposes. A made call reopened — one the person pulls up from the map, a message landing on decided ground — puts what the call rested on beside what has moved: one they pull up asks what the derivation missed; a message on decided ground says whether the call still holds.

## Where the Ball Sits

The point ends by saying what kind of reply moves things forward. One of three shapes:

- **A question**, where the position turns on something only the person holds. A literal question, composed as altitude prescribes: the situation the person would be in, then what the product is or does on each side — one line per side where there are sides to choose between, so a number or a word answers it — and the one question mark that asks. Each side names what the product does, never the number that tunes it. Sides that cannot be composed as product end states mean the point has no question for the person: it is material for the record, and the close takes the third shape instead.
- **An invitation to push back**, where a real choice exists — pointing at the load-bearing reason the opener already gave as what a different reading would have to move, so the invitation belongs to this problem rather than to politeness.
- **Nothing needs a call** — say so plainly and offer the pause: they are free to read and weigh in, and a word from them moves things on.

A dead stop is not an ending: a point that trails off after its position leaves the person unsure whether a reply is owed or the conversation broke. No keyed menu, no bundled follow-ups, no stock closer: "what do you think?" is never the ask, and a closing beat repeated verbatim across a walk or a mailbox reads as chrome, not a colleague — phrase it from the problem just put. The beat draws only on what the opener already said — reaching into the held-back depth for a concrete pivot is how the case leaks back in one clause at a time.

**The test**, before the point goes out — read it as the person will, cold, in one glance: they can picture the behaviour, they know where you stand, and they know what reply is wanted, with no code identifier, no slug, no report or topic named ahead of their situation, no tuning knob in front of them, named or numbered, the alternative's cost one clause with nothing spelled out after it, and no option the record already rejected. A point that fails is recomposed at altitude, never sent and explained after.
