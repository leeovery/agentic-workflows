# Natural Breaks

*Shared reference. Loaded by `ledger-discussion-process`, at its `## Instructions` section.*

---

Natural breaks are points in the conversation where introducing something new won't derail the current thread. Use this checklist when deciding whether to raise what is waiting — a background agent's return, a waiting message, a call of your own — or bring up points set aside.

This is guidance, not hard-enforced. Err toward NOT interrupting when uncertain — deferring one turn is cheap, interrupting an active thread is expensive.

## Signals That It IS a Natural Break

Any of these qualifies:

- The current thread landed — a point reached decided or converging
- The person just said "what's next?", "move on", "anything else?", "ok", "done", or similar navigation cues
- The person just raised a new topic themselves (a clear pivot away from the current thread)
- A decision just landed AND the exchange before it resolved your outstanding question
- The topic is about to close — the closing pass is next, or the session is wrapping up
- The person explicitly asked about background-agent state ("anything come back yet?", "any results?")
- The session just opened or resumed and no conversation thread is underway yet — a pending announcement lands here, before momentum builds, rather than falling to **When Uncertain**'s default

## Signals That It Is NOT a Natural Break

Any of these means defer:

- You asked the person a direct question in your previous response and their reply hasn't yet arrived
- A gate is waiting on the person's answer — what arrived is held until it is answered
- A point is actively exploring, or a thread is being dug, and you are mid-probe on a specific concern within it
- The person is mid-response to a question you initiated (said "hold on", "let me think", or has only partially answered)
- You are mid-synthesis or mid-summary and haven't closed out the current point
- The current exchange is the first turn of a newly started point or thread — momentum belongs there, not to a new announcement
- The person just raised a new concern that you haven't yet engaged with
- The person put off what is waiting ("later", "not now") in their most recent turn — treat the next few turns as continuation, not a fresh break. Raising it again on the very next turn would ignore their deferral. Wait until the conversation has genuinely moved on before raising it again.

## When Uncertain

Default to NOT interrupting. What is waiting persists — the agent's row in `agent scan`, the mailbox — and the next natural break reconsiders the same question.
