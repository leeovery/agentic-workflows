Triage works through the inbox: the ideas, bugs and quick-fixes noted down to come back to. Pick items, and I'll name others that fit with them.

w → Work starts on what's picked now, as one piece of work; discovery finds what kind of work it is as we talk.
r → Roadmap puts what's picked on the roadmap, on a horizon you name; I never pick one.
a → Archive takes what's picked out of the inbox and into the archived items.
c → Change adds items to what's picked, or takes some out; I'll ask which.
v → View lists the archived items, to restore one to the inbox or delete it for good.
b → Back leaves triage with nothing changed.

Ask → Ask anything about triage or the inbox
