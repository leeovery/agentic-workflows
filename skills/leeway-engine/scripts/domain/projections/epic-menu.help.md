An epic's menu is where its topics are picked up, one conversation at a time. The heading names the phase the epic is in, and the epic stays in it until everything in it is done; beneath it, how many topics there are and where each stands.

What waits on you comes first. Decided topics, and those cancelled or postponed to the roadmap, are listed above the menu and take no key.

# → A number opens what it names: something waiting on you, a topic to start or continue, the laboratory where an experiment waits for its topic, or the build or the review once ready to build's yes has begun the build. A topic struck through is open in another conversation; picking it asks before taking it over.
e → Verdicts appears while a call you changed waits for your word on what Claude's answer would have cost.
s → Spec appears once every topic is decided, cancelled or postponed, and starts or carries on the epic's one specification.
d → Discovery changes the map, until the spec is first written: a new topic, a cancel, a postpone to the roadmap, a topic pulled forward or reactivated, or a decided topic reopened.
b → Back returns to the start menu.

Ask → Ask anything about this epic or its menu
