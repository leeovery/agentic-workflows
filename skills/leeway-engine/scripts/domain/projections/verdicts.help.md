The verdict queue holds the calls you changed that still wait on your verdict: what the product would have lost had I built my answer instead of yours. Until you give one, each counts as a real loss, so a long queue only makes me ask you more. They come one at a time, oldest first.

n → Nothing says my answer would have served as well.
f → Fix says mine would have cost a fix later.
l → Loss says mine would have been a real loss.
b → Back leaves the rest waiting, for when you choose.

Ask → Ask anything about verdicts or the queue
