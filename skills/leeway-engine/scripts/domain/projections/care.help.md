A care entry names a kind of question that always comes to you in this project, however well my calls on it have matched yours. It is for what you care about here: what matters depends on what the project is for, so an entry never reaches another project. Your answers to its questions still count while it stands, so dropping it leaves the counts knowing how I do on that kind.

n → New adds a care entry: you pick the kind, from those with none here yet.
d → Drop takes a care entry away, and its kind follows the counts again, asking you by its rate as any kind does.
b → Back returns to the matrix.

Ask → Ask anything about care entries
