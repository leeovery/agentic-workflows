The roadmap holds what you've shaped but not yet committed to build, on horizons you name, in the order you put them; the first is read as next. Each item says what kind it is and where it came from. Name an item to see what it's about, or ask in words to rename one, change a horizon, or bring an archived item back.

w → Work starts on the items you name, as one piece of work; discovery finds what kind of work it is as we talk, and the rest stay waiting.
n → New adds an item: I'll ask what it is and which horizon it goes on; I never pick one.
m → Move puts an item on another horizon you name.
a → Archive takes an item off the roadmap; it can be brought back.
p → Product carries on the conversation about the product as a whole.
b → Back returns to the start menu.

Ask → Ask anything about the roadmap
