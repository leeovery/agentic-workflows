Changes

Tick a line you agree with, or comment on it. Claude answers beneath your comment, and you can reply as many times as you like, one message per turn; tick the line once it's settled. A tick closes the conversation, and unticking opens it again. Claude reads your comments when you tell it to: go back to your Claude session and say you've left comments. You can edit or remove a comment until Claude answers it; after that it stays as written. A tick agrees the line as it's worded: if the wording changes afterwards, the tick comes off and the line is marked changed. Keys: j k move between lines, t ticks, c comments or replies, n goes to the earliest line waiting on you, ⌘↵ sends, Esc closes.
