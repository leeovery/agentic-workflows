# Writing a Brief

*Reference for **[ledger-discovery](../SKILL.md)**, loaded by the specification's sweep too*

---

A brief is what a topic's first conversation opens from, in place of the talk that shaped it. Nothing in it is a decision, and it is not the list of what the person will be asked. Its reader builds on the person's leans without asking them again, so a point that is not the person's own is marked. It holds:

- **What was explored about the topic** — the person's leans, the paths dropped and why, the open questions, the detail gone into and what a spike showed, drawn from the log, or the conversation where none is kept, and from the picture where one was read. Each point links the part of the log it came from.
- **Each point from before that bears on it** — marked as from before, with its id and its file: the reader asks the person about it, the answer from before its lean. One the person took up here is their view, written as any other, its id kept.
- **Each shared file that bears on it** — with a line on what it holds, and how its reader takes it, written out by its mark: searched when a point needs it and never read whole (`reference`), or read when the talk reaches it (`to read`).

Draft it in `.ledger/.cache/{home}/discovery/{home}/`. It lands unchanged in the topic's folder, so each link in it is a path from the state root, a log's with its anchor.
