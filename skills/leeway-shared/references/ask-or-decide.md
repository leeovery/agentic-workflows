# Ask or Decide

*Shared reference. Loaded by `framework.md`.*

---

Which calls never become a question, and which go to `raise`. Whether a raise reaches the person is the engine's to decide, never yours: you supply the question and your lean. A phase that works its calls through in the conversation for a time says so, and there they are asked in the conversation instead. [voice.md](voice.md) governs how a challenge sounds.

## Facts Are Checked

A question that reading the code, reading the record or running something can answer never reaches the person: check it.

Where there is more than one way and no obvious choice, find out before you lean. When each way has its pros, cons and risks and evidence can tell them apart, spike it, research it, read or reverse-engineer the source, or look at how open-source products do it, rather than guess or ask. That runs on a disposable machine or this one, without the person; in a work unit, what it shows lands through `ledger find` for the call to rely on.

## Every Other Call Goes to `raise`

Every call you can't settle by checking goes to `raise`, the obvious ones included, however sure you are: what the person wants the product to be and do, and equally a data model, a contract, the code's insides or the process, and a fact only the person holds, such as a deadline or an outside constraint. Compose it product first where it can be; never settle it alone.

In the raise you say whether the call has one defensible answer or several. It has one where the record — decisions already made, sibling work, the brief — platform or industry convention, or first principles admit exactly one; it has several where the answer turns on what the person wants or knows.

## Answering a Raise

- **A pick, or the person's own answer,** goes to `raise answer`, never to `gate`.
- **`e/explain`** is no answer: run `raise answer {id} --didnt-follow`, explain it again, and the question stays open.
- **`t/technical`** draws the raise again with its mechanism shown, `raise show {id} --technical`, or hidden, `raise show {id}`.
- **A comment or a question** at a raise is talked through, then the raise is drawn again with `raise show {id}`.

Questions reach the person one at a time. In a conversation each slots in at a natural pause; where several wait at once they come back to back, each answered before the next shows.
