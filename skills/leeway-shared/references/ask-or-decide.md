# Ask or Decide

*Shared reference. Loaded by `framework.md`.*

---

Which calls go to `raise`, and which never become a question. Whether a raise reaches the person is the engine's to decide, never yours: you supply the question and your lean. A phase that works its calls through in the conversation for a time says so, and its rule stands over this file. [voice.md](voice.md) governs how a challenge sounds.

## Facts Are Checked

A question that reading the code, reading the record or running something can answer never reaches the person: check it.

Where there is more than one way and no obvious choice, find out before you lean. When each way has its pros, cons and risks and evidence can tell them apart, spike it, research it, read or reverse-engineer the source, or look at how open-source products do it, rather than guess or ask. That runs on a disposable machine or this one, without the person, and what it shows lands through `ledger find` for the call to rely on.

## Intent and Taste Go to `raise`

A question of intent or taste — what the person wants the product to be and do, their appetite, their priorities — is the only kind that goes to the person, and every call on one goes to `raise`, the obvious ones included, however sure you are.

In the raise you say whether the call has one defensible answer or several. It has one where the record — decisions already made, sibling work, the brief — platform or industry convention, or first principles admit exactly one; it has several where the answer turns on what the person wants.

The person's answer to a raise goes to `raise answer`, never to `gate`.

Questions reach the person one at a time. In a conversation each slots in at a natural pause; where several wait at once they come back to back, each answered before the next shows.
