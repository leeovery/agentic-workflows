# Areas: a piece of work as one record

A design in discussion. Nothing here is built. "Area" is a working name.

Each line ends with where it stands: **[agreed]** the person said yes to it, with the date; **[proposed]** put to the person and not objected to, never yet a yes; **[open]** not settled.

## 1. Why

1.1 The aim: "getting as close to that raw Claude feeling as possible, but while still having the benefit of Ledger… it's there, but it's behind the scenes. And the only time the user really sees it is the times when it makes sense." Topics are "maybe the only part that's left… that keeps some of the old system". [the person, 25 Nov 2026]

1.2 What topics cost, measured on a project of 17 topics over 100 days: completed topics were reopened 57 times, 55 of them by a message from another topic. A message cost a reopen only when its receiver was already completed: 63 messages to completed topics caused 55 reopens; 121 to topics in progress or not started caused none. Four topics that were one decision space exchanged 49 of 184 messages and hold over half the words. Twelve topics were cut at the start; passes reading across them later added eight for ground no topic owned.

1.3 What a large product designed with no topics showed: it grew about twenty units anyway, none chosen up front, and only six of fourteen could have been named on the first day. Gaps appeared where two units were designed at once in separate conversations, rarely where one followed another. Almost every rule is stated in one place, with pointers from the rest. Its one large design document strained at its size.

1.4 So the unit is kept and three things attached to it go: it is cut up front; it is shut on the person's yes, so what arrives later reopens it; and talk across units is forbidden, so everything crosses by message.

## 2. What stays

2.1 A call that reaches the person is drawn by the engine with its rows; now and then one is asked blind; a call of Claude's the person changed gets a verdict; a close shows its summary. "Those should stay. They are integral to the trust system and should loosen as the trust system takes more." [agreed, 24 Nov 2026]

2.2 Inside a focused conversation the work runs as a topic's does today: the shape is agreed with the person first, then Claude fills in through the ask rule; the map shows where each point stands, Claude's calls since the person last looked first; every open question holds the close. [agreed, 25 Nov 2026]

2.3 The phases, the menus where they make sense, and the formal process of the build and its reviews. [agreed, 25 Nov 2026]

2.4 Nothing from discovery goes on the ledger. The ledger is what discussion formalises. [agreed, 25 Nov 2026]

2.5 Where protection is needed the engine holds it; prose alone never does. [agreed, 25 Nov 2026]

2.6 No single large document is ever the working surface. The record is entries, and what a conversation reads is a view the engine draws. [agreed, 25 Nov 2026]

## 3. The area

3.1 An area is a label: on a piece of work's entries, and on the points of its starting page (6.3). It has no folder, no brief and no mailbox. [agreed, 25 Nov 2026]

3.2 Areas are never cut up front. The first grouping is Claude's, proposed in the talk from what the starting page holds, and changed by the person saying so. Nothing is approved. Where little or nothing came across, the work starts with few areas or none, and they form from the talk. [agreed, 25 Nov 2026]

3.3 An area forms, splits or folds into another as the talk shows the seams: Claude offers it in a line and the person says yes, or the person says it. [agreed, 25 Nov 2026]

3.4 A relabel is a line appended to the ledger naming the entries that move. Nothing is edited; every view reads an entry where its latest such line put it; the trail keeps where it was first recorded and each move since. [agreed, 25 Nov 2026]

3.5 Every entry has one owner: an area, or the work itself for a rule that cuts across areas. [proposed]

3.6 Where to start is Claude's suggestion, by what rests on what. No order is approved. [proposed]

## 4. Conversations

4.1 A broad conversation is about the whole piece of work. It can go to any area, and one area can be filled in by talk about the others. [agreed, 25 Nov 2026]

4.2 A focused conversation is on one area. The person asks for it, or Claude suggests it in a line — the talk keeps returning to one area, an area has gathered open questions, a question needs more depth than a broad conversation should spend — and the person says yes. It never switches by itself. [proposed]

4.3 A focused conversation opens as a clean session by a handoff, scoped by the engine: it reads its area's part of the starting page, its area's entries and the decisions about the whole, and nothing else unless it goes looking. One session holds an area at a time; several areas may be open in several windows. [proposed; the clean scoped session is the person's, 25 Nov 2026]

4.4 In a focused conversation Claude's calls land in its own area alone: the engine refuses another. A point for another area is recorded as a question under that area, with the reasoning, and the conversation carries on. A decision of the person's lands in the area it belongs to, directly, wherever it was said. [proposed]

4.5 A question under another area is answered at once where the conversation is broad. In a focused conversation it is the person's choice: answered there where it is theirs to decide and quick, otherwise left. Left, it shows on the map under its area, in that area's count on the work's menu, first at the next focused conversation there, and it holds the spec. It changes nothing about the area it lands on. [proposed]

4.6 Each sitting keeps one log, numbered as discovery's are, marked broad or focused on an area, written in sections named for what they cover. Every entry links the section it came from. An area's history is its focused sittings' logs and the linked sections of the broad ones. [agreed, 25 Nov 2026]

## 5. Closing an area

5.1 Where an area has no open question and the talk has rested, Claude offers to close it. The close is as a topic's today: the fresh reader's pass, the summary, a verdict on each call the person changed, the person's yes. [proposed]

5.2 A closed area stays closed. A question that arrives later is added under it and answered; the area is not reopened and not closed again. The pass that reads across areas before the spec checks that nothing was disturbed. [proposed]

5.3 An area needing no depth is closed from a broad conversation. [proposed]

## 6. From product discovery into a piece of work

6.1 Product discovery is as today: each session's log in named sections, the picture kept current, roadmap items with their notes and links to the log. [agreed, 25 Nov 2026]

6.2 The person commits to a piece of work from the roadmap items they choose, at the start screen, as today. [agreed, 25 Nov 2026]

6.3 The work gets one starting page: the picture's detail on it, moved over — the person's leans, the paths dropped and why, the open questions, what the spikes showed — each point linked to its log section. One page for the work, never one per area. [agreed, 25 Nov 2026]

6.4 No session holds weeks of discovery, so reader agents read every linked log section in full against the page, and each reports what the page misses or gets wrong, with its link. [proposed]

6.5 The engine checks the links: every log section the work was shaped by is cited by a point on the page, or marked as carrying nothing. [proposed]

6.6 Each point on the page has an id the engine tracks. A point reaches the ledger only when the talk takes it up: a lean the person confirms or changes becomes their decision; an open one becomes a question on the map. [proposed]

6.7 The map shows, by area, the points from discovery not yet taken up. [proposed]

## 7. Ready for the spec

Held by the engine, which refuses the spec until each holds:

7.1 Every area is closed, or put off. [proposed]

7.2 No question is open anywhere on the map. [proposed]

7.3 The pass that reads across areas has run since the last decision, and everything it raised is closed. It may run more than once. It proposes what is missing — an area nobody has talked through, a point that falls between two — and each is then worked like any other. [proposed]

7.4 No point from discovery is left: each is on the ledger, or dropped at the person's word. [proposed]

## 8. What goes

8.2 Messages between topics and their mailboxes. [proposed]

8.3 Reopening. [proposed]

## 9. One kind of work

9.1 A feature and an epic become one kind: a feature is a piece of work that never needed a second area. "Whether through conversation we develop additional lenses or not is a product of the conversation that happens and the size of the work rather than some arbitrary upfront label." [the person's thought, 25 Nov 2026; open]

9.2 A bugfix and a quick-fix stay kinds of their own: their paths differ. [proposed]
