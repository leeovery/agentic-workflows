# Fixture — epic-menu-postpones-a-decided-topic

The `search-relevance` epic with its thinking half done: three topics on
a sequenced map (`behavioural-ranking` 1, `synonym-handling` 2,
`relevance-measurement` 3), the first two discussed and decided, the
third never started. No specification has been proposed over either
discussion, no experiment record exists, and no triage queue holds
anything — so each topic's Discovery unit is its map row and whatever
conversation sits under it.

`synonym-handling` is decided but not this release's: the platform team
has a managed search product landing next year, and the expansion work
belongs beside it rather than in front of it. Nothing about the decision
is wrong — it is simply not now.

The project has no roadmap. Nothing has ever been parked onto one and no
work unit was ever pulled from one, so there are no horizons to choose
between and the roadmap itself does not yet exist.
