# Fixture — feature-discussion-cancels-the-unit

The `pay` feature — accept card payments at checkout — shaped in
discovery and one sitting into its discussion. Two subtopics sit on the
map: capture confirmation is decided and written up (gateway webhooks,
never polling), and what the shopper sees when a card is declined is
still being explored.

A feature's topic is its work unit, so nothing sits above the name and
nothing beside it: no discovery map row, no sibling topic, no research,
and nothing downstream of the discussion. The work unit is in progress
and it is the only one on the project.

The context was cleared at the sitting's end — this session opens cold
at the entry skill with nothing but its two arguments and what is on
disk.
