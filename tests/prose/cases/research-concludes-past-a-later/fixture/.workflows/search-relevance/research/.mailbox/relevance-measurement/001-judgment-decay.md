### Do click-derived judgments decay faster for expanded queries?
*From: query-expansion · discussion · 2026-09-10*

Query expansion rewrites a shopper's words before ranking, so the
judgments collected under the old wording may not describe the
expanded query at all. The expansion discussion cannot decide how
aggressively to expand without knowing whether the measurement
harness will read a rewritten query against stale judgments — a
question for the measurement research, not a decision for expansion.
