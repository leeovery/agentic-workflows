# Fixture — epic-menu-restores-a-postponed-topic

The `search-relevance` epic with its thinking half done and one topic
already gone: three topics were sequenced on the map
(`behavioural-ranking` 1, `synonym-handling` 2, `relevance-measurement`
3), the first two discussed and decided, the third never started — and
then `synonym-handling` was postponed to the roadmap under a `v2`
horizon, because the platform team had a managed search product landing
next year.

The postpone is the only thing that has happened since: the map row
carries its marker with its order stashed, the concluded discussion sits
stashed beneath it, its file untouched on disk, and the project's
roadmap — created by that postpone — holds one waiting item under `v2`
recording where it came from.

The managed search product has now slipped a release. The expansion work
is this epic's again.
