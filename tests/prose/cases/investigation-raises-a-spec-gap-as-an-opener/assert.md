The prose should have taken this path:

1. the entry reads the investigation status — in-progress, reopened by
   the triage delivery — emits the resuming phase note, runs no
   interview and no reopen, and hands off with source: existing
   investigation
2. the process finds the investigation file, reads the queue (one
   entry) and renders resume detection — the gate with the one-concern
   triage warning directly above its menu — and the user continues;
   initialisation is skipped
3. the knowledge guidance loads without a query; symptom gathering is
   skipped — an earlier session interviewed the user and this one has
   nothing new to fold in — and the triage check, on its first
   consult of a resumed sitting with a non-empty queue, renders the
   one-entry agenda — title and origin only, body unread — with the
   offer menu before any session output, and stops for the user
4. the user opts in; the raise reads the queue file as the session's
   own brief; the provenance line names this topic's own name, so the
   origin binds to the specification, never the topic to itself; no
   requeue offer renders (an investigation has no other phase-side)
   and no map is armed (there is no Discussion Map); the entry carries
   one ask, so it is one raise
5. the raise is composed through the shared opener contract from the
   entry and emitted in the same turn, and the turn ends awaiting the
   user, where the walk stops — the contextual query, the step after
   the triage check, never runs

Presentation claims — the opener shape from an investigation is the
behaviour under test:

- the first sentence names the shop's customer and their moment — a
  customer paying for a download with a wallet, reaching the payment
  step, or the like — and names no topic, phase, specification, queue,
  entry, or concern
- where it came from arrives in a clause only after the situation is
  on the page, and it names the specification — the phase that could
  not be written without this — never `crash-fix` as though it were a
  sibling topic that sent the concern to itself, and never as
  something the specification "settled": a spec gap settled nothing
- the problem lands through one to three devices — a worked instance
  (the customer with a typed billing address checking out fine, the
  wallet payer with none failing one address further along), a
  before/after, a small diagram — chosen for understanding-speed,
  never all of them
- the raise states a position — a lean between asking the customer
  for their country before payment and working the location out from
  what the checkout already holds — with one load-bearing reason
  riding as a clause, never its derivation; never a neutral option
  survey and never an abdication; the alternative gets at most one
  clause naming the kind of cost it carries — never two costs, never a
  cost with its consequence spelled out
- no experiment and no research pass is proposed, and the entry's note
  that the conversion cost was never measured is not turned into a
  proposal to measure it: a bugfix carries neither instrument, and the
  raise's position is a lean or a question, never "let's find out"
- the entry's tuning stays back: the issuer lookup's budget (a quarter
  of a second), the tax lookup's timeout (eight hundred milliseconds)
  and the retry count (two attempts) appear nowhere in the raise; the
  order shares — the wallet share and the saved-card share — may reach
  the user only as what they mean for the product (a good share of
  these orders carry no address at all), with or without their
  figures, and never as a figure weighed against a budget
- the rejected zero-rating alternative appears nowhere in the raise
- the raise's last beat says where the ball sits — a literal question
  whose sides are product end states (the checkout asks a wallet payer
  for their country before taking payment, or it works the location
  out and never asks), or an invitation to push back pointing at the
  reason the raise already gave — never "what do you think?", no keyed
  menu, no bundled follow-ups, never a dead stop after the position,
  and nothing drawn from the held-back depth
- the raise's prose runs to about three hundred words at most, a
  diagram aside — the length of a raise that lands, against an entry
  of 714 words — readable in a glance — and the entry is never emitted
  verbatim: no paragraph of it appears in the turn
- the raise covers this concern alone: no other item, finding, or gap
  rides along
- no outcome is documented in the raise's turn: nothing is written to
  the investigation file, no direction is recorded, no fold runs

Further claims:

- no requeue offer, no absorb, no completion, no agent dispatch, no
  experiment spawned
- the investigation file and the specification file are byte-unchanged
  and no commit lands past the fixture's (render payloads and per-turn
  presence heartbeats under `.workflows/.cache/` are machinery, not
  writes)
- the queue file is still present with its content intact
- the manifest is untouched: the investigation item still reads
  in-progress, the specification item still in-progress with its
  source row pending
- the walk stops with the raise pending; the user never answers it

EXPECTED WORLD — the fixture, unchanged. An investigation arms no map
and the engine stamps nothing durable on this path — `topic queue`
only refreshes a heartbeat — so the only additions are machinery: the
triage-offer payload and any presence heartbeats under
`.workflows/.cache/`.
