The `search-relevance` epic. The `synonym-handling` research is
mid-flight and paused on evidence twice over: it narrowed the
replacement question to a curated managed list versus behaviour-driven
expansion, then handed the laboratory two numbers the choice turns on —
E1, what share of zero-result searches recover by in-session
reformulation, and E2, how quickly those recoveries land. Both records
are conceived, each holding its problem statement and nothing else; the
research item awaits both.

A day of sandbox search-session activity sits in the repo at
`logs/search-sessions.log` — forty zero-result searches, twelve of
which recover with a clicked reformulation in the same session, every
recovery landing thirty seconds after the failed query.

No other session is mid-flight. The context was cleared at the pause —
this session opens cold at the experiment entry with its four
arguments and what is on disk.
