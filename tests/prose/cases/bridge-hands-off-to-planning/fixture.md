# Fixture — bridge-hands-off-to-planning

The `pay` feature stands with its discussion completed and nothing
beyond it — the same starting world as the specification case: three
recorded decisions (existing gateway account, card-only v1 with
wallets deferred, webhook-confirmed capture with no polling) and one
deferral.

The walk that concludes this specification should reach the bridge
with `planning` as the derived next phase and `discussion` and
`specification` behind it as revisit candidates — the state where the
revisit gate and the plan-mode handoff genuinely fire.
